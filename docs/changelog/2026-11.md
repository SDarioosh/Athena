## Done on 2026-11-03: identify who spoke a command (MCP 0.22.0)

- `POST /v1/speakers/identify` (`server/athena/speaker_identify.py`): multipart `audio` (a
  voice satellite's spoken command) → `{"speaker": {id, name, is_owner} | null, "score",
  "threshold", "speech_seconds"}`. VAD speech, the diarizer's speaker embedder and the
  enrolled voices, scored like diarization's matching; threshold
  `CONVO_SPEAKER_IDENTIFY_THRESHOLD` (unset: `CONVO_SPEAKER_MATCH_THRESHOLD`, 0.50).
  `speaker` is null below it or with less than 1 s of speech. At most 2 MB and 20 s. The
  audio is never stored or logged, nothing is written to the database.
- Relay route `POST /speakers/identify` on the MCP HTTP app (forwarded unchanged), for the
  hub's voice satellite check (`FORK_VOICE_PRIVATE=owner` answers personal questions only
  for the owner's voice). MCP 0.22.0, additive. Contract in `docs/api/ingest.md`.
