# T-Display K230: board facts for the PDA

What the image and the programs rely on, and where each fact comes from. Nothing here has
been checked on a board yet (the sandbox has none). **CONFIRMED** = read in the source
named (LilyGO's [T-Display-K230](https://github.com/Xinyuan-LilyGO/T-Display-K230) at
c27c58c, its `k230_linux_sdk` submodule at fc6cc21, Buildroot 2025.02.1, the kernel's
`k230_defconfig` at ruyisdk/linux-xuantie-kernel 7d4e1f4); **GUESS** = inferred, to check
on the board (the "Not verified yet" list in [../README.md](../README.md) has the steps).

Paths below: `bsp/` = `k230_bsp/overlay/buildroot-overlay/`, `launcher/` =
`k230_launcher/k230_phone_ui/src/` (both in LilyGO's repo), `sdk/` =
`k230_linux_sdk/buildroot-overlay/`.

## Battery and charger

**Not in sysfs on this image.** The BQ27220 gauge (0x55) and the BQ25896 charger (0x6B) are
on LilyGO's optional nRF9151 keyboard base, I2C4 on GPIO46/47; there is no
`power_supply` driver for them (no `BATTERY_BQ27XXX`/`CHARGER_BQ25890` in the kernel config
or fragments), so `/sys/class/power_supply` is empty and the UI's `power_read` finds no
battery. The launcher reads them by bit-banging GPIO46/47 through `/dev/gpiochip1` lines 14/15
after switching the pins with `/dev/mem` (root only).

| What | Fact | Source | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Addresses, registers | BQ27220 0x55: SoC 0x2C, voltage 0x08; BQ25896 0x6B: status 0x0B (bits 4:3 charge state, bit 2 power good) | `launcher/ui_hardware.c:144,151,184,186,198`; `k230_bsp/docs/HARDWARE_PINMAP.md:121-124` | CONFIRMED |
| Bus | the launcher's bit-bang: `keyboard_i2c_begin` | `launcher/ui_hardware.c:132,136,2648` | CONFIRMED |
| Kernel bus | `&i2c4` is enabled in the device tree; LilyGO's I2C scan calls Linux bus 0 "physical I2C4", so `i2cget -y 0 0x55 0x2c w` may read the gauge | `bsp/linux/0032-riscv-dts-canaan-add-k230-rm69a10-dts.patch:47`; `launcher/ui_i2c_scan.c:54` | GUESS |

`athena-board battery` tries the kernel bus (root, BusyBox `i2cget`). Status flags bit1/bit2
(charging, USB) stay 0 until one of these works; a root helper or a DT/driver change would be
the follow-up.
